\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Complex spatiotemporal systems are often forecast with black-box predictors whose
internal propagation rules are not identifiable. We cast local evolution as a
unified physical computational primitive
\(\hat{X}=P(X;G,\Theta,\alpha)\), in which wave-like, instantaneous and
diffusive couplings are mechanism coordinates \(\Theta\) rather than unrelated
models; forecast error is only a probe; and \(\alpha\) is a local deviation from
a domain coordinate \(\Theta^{\star}\). On the \stead{} seismic benchmark, the
preferred coordinate is Huygens/wave. A physics-constrained probe with
\(192\,\mathrm{k}\) parameters matches \eqt{} on detection F1, improves S-wave
F1, and achieves the lowest P-wave timing error (P MAE \(0.0169\,\mathrm{s}\)
versus \(0.0462\,\mathrm{s}\) / \(0.0723\,\mathrm{s}\) for \eqt{} / \pnet{};
Table~\ref{tab:stead}). Under the same prior we assign each local-earthquake
waveform to one of five frozen, interpretable coda facies and map a structure
residual \(\beta_{\mathrm{res}}\) that separates path domains not reducible to
scalar coda \(Q_c\). Scalp EEG and NOAA SST select different coordinates
(instantaneous; graph diffusion), and a telegrapher-inspired path
\(\Theta(\lambda)\) exhibits a damped-wave intermediate between wave and
diffusion. Finally, \(\alpha\) is geometry-bound under EEG counterfactuals and
admits full frozen reinjection closure on SST (partial on EEG range-dependent
gain). Local propagation is therefore an identifiable cross-domain computational
object, with its deepest empirical thickness in seismic Huygens facies.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Introduction}
% =============================================================================

Scientific machine learning faces a persistent fork. Purely data-driven networks
can score well on detection and next-step prediction, yet their internal
propagation rules are rarely identifiable, so high forecast scores seldom
translate into trustworthy instruments of discovery. Physics-informed penalties
improve consistency only approximately. Hybrid ``explainable'' heads often
attach post-hoc probes to opaque backbones, so the explanation remains
correlative rather than constitutive of the computation.

Four linked gaps follow. First, across spatiotemporal systems one cannot tell
whether a domain prefers delayed wave coupling, instantaneous geometric
coupling, or diffusion, because models are usually rebuilt as unrelated
architectures rather than compared under a matched computational budget.
Second, seismology already has mature velocity and attenuation maps, yet coda
\emph{how a path rings} is still routinely collapsed to a scalar quality factor
\(Q_c\), leaving no interpretable discrete language for multi-lobe delay
structure and path-domain leftovers. Third, scalp EEG and climate sensor
networks raise the dual question---do they share that delayed-wave language, or
different coordinates of one family?---without a protocol that can falsify the
answer. Fourth, even when a multiplicative deviation field \(\alpha\) is fit to
absorb residual error, forecast improvement alone does not establish mechanism:
without geometry counterfactuals and, where claimed, frozen reinjection,
\(\alpha\) remains an untested fit residue.

A sharper requirement is therefore needed. Known local propagation physics
should enter as a \emph{differentiable computational primitive}---a readable
Green's-function-like operator that interrogates data---while still allowing
domains to differ in \emph{which} propagation regime they select. Forecast
error is only a probe; the scientific objects are the preferred mechanism
coordinate \(\Theta^{\star}\) and any validated local deviation \(\alpha\) from
that coordinate.

Here we formulate recursive local evolution
\begin{equation}
\text{local state}\rightarrow
\text{local response}\rightarrow
\text{neighbourhood messages }(A_{ij},\tau_{ij})\rightarrow
\text{secondary sources}
\end{equation}
as one operator \(\hat{X}=P(X;G,\Theta,\alpha)\) (Fig.~\ref{fig:method}).
Geometry \(G\) supplies distances and sampling; structural parameters \(\Theta\)
continuously interpolate delay, spatial kernel family, normalisation and
travel-time damping; \(\alpha\) multiplies the kernel at a domain coordinate
\(\Theta^{\star}\). Wave/Huygens, instantaneous coupling, continuum diffusion
and graph diffusion are named corners of \(\Theta\), not three unrelated
architectures. A one-parameter telegrapher-inspired path \(\Theta(\lambda)\)
connects wave (\(\lambda{=}0\)) to diffusion (\(\lambda{=}1\)) through a
damped-wave intermediate. In seismology the same Huygens corner is instantiated
as a multi-scale Fresnel probe for picking, then reused---without retuning
ceilings to geography---as an instrument for frozen coda facies and structure
residuals.

We make three linked contributions. First, on \stead{} a Huygens-type probe is
competitive with \eqt{} and \pnet{} on the full test set while remaining
physically structured (Table~\ref{tab:stead}), and the same probe supports a
five-class frozen coda facies taxonomy whose structure residual maps path
domains beyond scalar \(Q_c\) (Fig.~\ref{fig:seismic}). Second, matched-budget
ablations place scalp EEG and NOAA SST at different \(\Theta^{\star}\), and
\(\Theta(\lambda)\) behaves as a continuous dynamical space
(Fig.~\ref{fig:cross}; Table~\ref{tab:domains}). Third, \(\alpha\) is validated
as a geometry-bound deviation from domain \(\Theta^{\star}\) by counterfactuals
and, where successful, frozen reinjection (Fig.~\ref{fig:alpha}).

We do \emph{not} claim universal Huygens physics, a clinical EEG biomarker, or
that the five facies are precursors. Forecast metrics are usefulness evidence
under an interpretable prior, not the scientific endpoint.

\section*{Results}

\subsection*{A Huygens coordinate on STEAD: competitive timing under a physics-constrained probe}

We evaluate the multi-scale Huygens--Fresnel probe (\pnf{}/\hnf{} run family;
\(192\,\mathrm{k}\) parameters) on \stead{} under the standard
\(0.5\,\mathrm{s}\) tolerance protocol used for \eqt{}.
Table~\ref{tab:stead} reports full test-set detection and picking against
\eqt{} (\(\sim 377\,\mathrm{k}\) parameters) and \pnet{}
(\(\sim 269\,\mathrm{k}\)).

\pnf{} \textbf{matches \eqt{} on detection F1} (\(0.9989\)),
\textbf{improves S-wave F1} (\(0.9770\) vs.\ \(0.9731\) / \(0.9618\)), and
achieves the \textbf{lowest P-wave MAE}
(\(0.0169\,\mathrm{s}\) on the discrete \(800\)-bin/\(60\,\mathrm{s}\) grid;
Methods), roughly \(2.7\times\) tighter than \eqt{} (\(0.0462\,\mathrm{s}\))
and \(4.3\times\) tighter than \pnet{} (\(0.0723\,\mathrm{s}\)). It does not
dominate every cell: P-wave F1 (\(0.9852\)) trails \eqt{} (\(0.9897\)), and
S-wave MAE (\(0.0878\,\mathrm{s}\)) trails \pnet{} (\(0.0801\,\mathrm{s}\)).
Precision/Recall in Table~\ref{tab:stead} shows strong recall across heads
(P-wave R \(0.9763\), S-wave R \(0.9643\)): the physics-constrained architecture
reliably identifies genuine arrivals when they exist. Gaps to \eqt{} are driven
mainly by precision (PNF P-wave P \(0.9942\) vs.\ EQT \(0.9993\); S-wave P
\(0.9900\) vs.\ \(0.9994\)), consistent with an unconstrained transformer
trading physical structure for slightly tighter false-positive suppression. We
treat these differences as informative rather than disqualifying: a hard-wired
Huygens--Fresnel kernel restricts the hypothesis class relative to unconstrained
pickers, trading a fraction of peak picking score for readable latents that
enable the facies and path analyses below.

We read Table~\ref{tab:stead} as evidence that an interpretable Huygens-type
computational prior is \emph{not} purchased at a large timing cost---and on P
timing is markedly better---rather than as a claim of uniform state-of-the-art
across all transfer settings (OBS/adapt results are reported elsewhere and are
not used as primary claims here).

\subsection*{Five frozen coda facies and path-structure residuals}

\paragraph{Frozen facies---not post-hoc clusters.}
Internal probe states carry physics-indexed semantics. From a frozen checkpoint
we export causal-chain observables---coda slope, onset sharpness, and multi-lobe
structure in delay-time density \(\rho(\tau)\)---and assign each local-earthquake
waveform to one of five facies using ceilings fixed \emph{before} any regional
map was inspected (Fig.~\ref{fig:seismic}a; Supplementary Table~\ref{stab:ceilings}):
\textit{impulsive\_fast\_decay}, \textit{emergent}, \textit{multipath},
\textit{slow\_coda}, and \textit{standard}. These are not \(k\)-means labels.
Ceilings were chosen on validation to separate the three observables while
preserving interpretability, then frozen: peak-count threshold \(1.0\);
coda-fast/slow bounds \(-0.198\) / \(-0.113\); onset-high/low \(0.877\) /
\(0.733\). Because coda slope enters the ceilings, within-class coda contrasts
are facies characterisation, not independent physical validation.

The data basis is the \stead{} local-earthquake test catalogue (\(>100{,}000\)
traces after noise exclusion). Mapping facies geography---a quantity that did
not enter the ceilings---shows continental organisation (Cram\'er's
\(V\approx0.37\), FDR-adjusted \(p<0.001\); Extended Data
Fig.~\ref{edfig:faciesgeo}): impulsive fast decay enriches in the western United
States and northeast Pacific, whereas slow coda concentrates in Central America
and the western Pacific. In a causal frame with P at \(\tau=0\) and S at
\(\tau=1\), class-mean envelopes and \(\rho(\tau)\) separate cleanly: impulsive
fast decay combines sharp onset with short coda; emergent rises gradually;
multipath shows multiple \(\rho(\tau)\) lobes; slow coda sustains energy past
S; standard sits between these extremes.

\paragraph{Path-midpoint leftovers.}
Coda slope is distance-detrended to \(\beta\) against
\(\log_{10}(\mathrm{dist}+1)\). A ridge expectation (\(\lambda=8\)) removes
\(\log_{10}\) distance, depth, magnitude, log-distance to nearest Quaternary
fault, and station fixed effects; residual \(\beta_{\mathrm{res}}\) is mapped at
path midpoints. In southern California (fixed stations; \(20\)--\(120\,\mathrm{km}\);
structure model \(R^{2}\approx0.27\)), two cells illustrate opposite path
behaviour under same-station controls (Fig.~\ref{fig:seismic}b--d):
Salton--Imperial midpoints decay faster than the same stations' other paths
(site-median \(\Delta\beta\approx-0.032\); \(20\) stations; Cohen's \(d\approx-0.54\);
Mann--Whitney \(p=6.6\times10^{-32}\)), whereas eastern Transverse Ranges
midpoints ring longer (\(\Delta\beta\approx+0.025\); \(15\) stations;
\(d\approx+0.68\); \(p=2.5\times10^{-12}\)).

Independence checks (Extended Data Fig.~\ref{edfig:independence}) show:
(i)~classical single-trace \(q_c\) correlates with \(\beta_{\mathrm{res}}\)
(\(r=0.64\)), yet residualising on \(q_c\) halves but does not remove cell
anomalies (Salton \(\Delta\beta\): \(-0.030\rightarrow-0.014\); Transverse Ranges:
\(+0.040\rightarrow+0.019\)), and multipath versus slow-coda still separate
inside matched \(q_c\) terciles; (ii)~Berg et al.\ upper-crust \(V_S\) is
near-null (\(r=-0.08\)); cells occupy the same \(V_S\) band with opposite
\(\beta_{\mathrm{res}}\)\cite{berg2021}; (iii)~Lin \& Jordan direct-wave \(Q_S\)
is near-orthogonal (\(r=-0.07\)); removing \(Q_S\) leaves \(\Delta\beta\)
essentially intact, and Salton can combine relatively high direct-wave \(Q_S\)
with faster coda decay\cite{lin2023}. Near-null Pearson correlations can also
arise from resolution mismatch or coda-versus-direct sensitivity; we claim
information \emph{not captured by the examined linear summaries}, not complete
physical independence from all structure.

\paragraph{Classical catalog-timed coda baseline.}
Recomputing the identical envelope slope with \stead{} catalog \(S\) picks on
\(n=4000\) SoCal traces (Methods) yields catalog--probe slope agreement
\(r=0.96\), \(\beta_{\mathrm{res}}\) map agreement \(r=0.93\), and essentially
unchanged same-station cell signs. Five-fold OOS \(R^{2}\) for predicting probe
\(\beta_{\mathrm{res}}\) from classical coda features is \(0.711\); adding
probe-specific latents (\(n_{\rho}\) peaks, onset sharpness, facies one-hots)
yields \(\Delta R^{2}\approx0\). We therefore report the spatial leftover as
\emph{classical coda geography that the Huygens probe reproduces}, not a uniquely
neural residual. Distinctive contributions remain the competitive structured
picker (Table~\ref{tab:stead}), the \(\rho(\tau)\)-indexed five-mode taxonomy, and
operational coda timing without a separate catalog.

\paragraph{Out-of-region consistency and a falsifiable prediction.}
The identical frozen pipeline on Cascadia recovers a Salton-signed faster-decay
residual near Mt St Helens--Cowlitz (\(8\) stations; site-median
\(\Delta\beta=-0.014\); \(88\%\) sign agreement; \(d=-0.30\);
\(p=5.6\times10^{-14}\); jackknife STABLE) and an opposite ringing residual near
Seattle (\(\Delta\beta=+0.045\); \(d=+0.83\); Extended Data
Fig.~\ref{edfig:cascadia}). We present these as consistency examples, not a fully
pre-registered spatial replication catalog. A pre-registered-style prediction:
new paths with midpoints in the Salton cell should show same-station
\(\Delta\beta\le-0.02\) once \(\ge8\) stations are available; Seattle predicts
the opposite inequality.

\subsection*{Cross-domain mechanism coordinates and a continuous dynamical space}

\paragraph{Matched-budget regime identity.}
Under matched training budgets, local-response capacity and geometry controls,
scalp EEG prefers \textbf{instantaneous} coupling (lag \(\equiv 0\)) over delayed
Huygens, whereas NOAA SST sensor networks prefer \textbf{graph diffusion}
(Fig.~\ref{fig:cross}a; Table~\ref{tab:domains}). On merged EEG regime
ablations the instantaneous prior reaches mean MSE \(\approx0.064\) with
geometry-relative degradation \(\mathrm{rel}_{\mathrm{geom}}\approx+15\%\);
graph diffusion is second and delayed wave is worse. On SST sensor networks the
preferred prior is \texttt{graph\_diffusion}
(\(\mathrm{rel}_{\mathrm{geom}}\approx0.76\); diffusion-family preferred).
\stead{} Phase-1 already closed delayed wave/Huygens over free adjacency (weak
geometry-sensitive \(\alpha\); travel-time residual map claim falsified).

\paragraph{Unified corners and empirical \(\Theta^{\star}\) instances.}
Evaluating the unified operator at named corners recovers legacy kernels
(\texttt{exact} versus \texttt{unified} backends in parity;
\texttt{unified\_special\_case\_v1/}) and reproduces the EEG/SST preferences as
empirical \(\Theta^{\star}\) instances under \(\alpha\equiv1\) probes
(\texttt{unified\_empirical\_instances\_v1/}: SST\(\rightarrow\)graph\_diffusion,
EEG\(\rightarrow\)instantaneous; backend preferred parity YES;
Fig.~\ref{fig:cross}b). Piecewise continuum paths among named corners are
continuous enough under amplitude/MSE step gates
(\texttt{unified\_continuum\_v1/}). \stead{}'s Huygens landing, EEG's
instantaneous landing and SST's graph-diffusion landing therefore appear as
distinct coordinates of one family rather than three unrelated model classes.
Synthetic systems with known ground-truth propagators recover perfect
confusion-matrix accuracy under an \(\alpha\equiv1\) decision rule
(\texttt{synth\_regime\_ident\_v1/}; taxonomy gate T1), establishing
identifiability in principle; real-domain score-matrix and leave-one-domain
fingerprint gates remain open (Extended Data).

\paragraph{Telegrapher path \(\Theta(\lambda)\).}
A telegrapher-inspired path \(\Theta(\lambda)\) with delay
\(\delta=(1-\lambda)^{2}\), heat mix \(\eta=\lambda^{2}\) and travel-time
damping \(\zeta=4\lambda(1-\lambda)\) connects wave (\(\lambda{=}0\)) to
diffusion (\(\lambda{=}1\)). On the primary wave\(\rightarrow\)diffusion path,
mean lag collapses monotonically (\(0.523\rightarrow0\)); mid-path damping
peaks; phase labels traverse wave-like \(\rightarrow\) damped-wave
\(\rightarrow\) diffusive (\texttt{dynamical\_space\_pass=YES},
\texttt{visits\_damped\_wave=YES}; Fig.~\ref{fig:cross}c,d;
\texttt{unified\_theta\_lambda\_v1/}). This supports the stronger claim that
regimes form a \textbf{continuous dynamical space}, not only a discrete
dictionary. Multi-primitive mixtures \(\sum_k\alpha_k K_k\) are deliberately
paused (non-identifiability risk) and are not claimed here.

\subsection*{$\alpha$ as local deviation from domain $\Theta^{\star}$}

We reinterpret \(\alpha\) as a multiplicative deviation from \(K(\Theta^{\star})\),
not as a universal residual relative to Huygens in every domain.

\paragraph{EEG geometry-bound \(\alpha\) (harden + LEMON).}
Under EEG's instantaneous \(\Theta^{\star}\), a low-dimensional
distance-structured \(\alpha\) is necessary relative to \(\alpha\equiv1\)
(gate G1) and degrades under wrong-geometry and edge-permutation
counterfactuals: CF \(\alpha\) on scrambled geometry raises relative error by
\(\approx+82\%\) (G4); permuting \(\alpha\) edges raises it by \(\approx+64\%\)
(G6); distance-slope sign is seed-stable (G7 agree \(=1.0\);
\texttt{eeg\_instantaneous\_harden\_v1/}; five seeds; subject bootstrap;
Fig.~\ref{fig:alpha}a). An independent healthy-lifespan LEMON cohort
reproduces the mechanism pattern: instantaneous MSE \(0.076\) versus wave
\(0.098\); wrong-geometry CF relative \(\approx+67\%\) (CI \([0.37,1.09]\),
\(90\) subjects; \texttt{replication\_pass=YES};
Fig.~\ref{fig:alpha}b). Forecast MSE alone is not the claim: free/local
baselines can beat our MSE under unfair weight-permutation metrics; mechanism
claims rest on geometry-bound CF gates (Methods; Extended Data).

\paragraph{SST full RDG reinjection versus EEG partial transfer.}
On SST under graph diffusion, a mined range-dependent gain (RDG) freezes on
held-out seeds and passes named-versus-wrong reinjection closure without
refitting mechanism parameters (H1c\(^\prime\), \(\ell=0.5\);
\texttt{closure\_pass=YES}; Fig.~\ref{fig:alpha}c;
\texttt{sst\_alpha\_rdg\_closure\_v3/}). A companion physics-consistency map
aligns near-boost / mid-cut structure with SST contrast, with the explicit
caveat that the scale is the sensor graph, not geophysical Rd
(\texttt{sst\_rdg\_physics\_v1/}). The same RDG family only partially tracks
free \(\alpha\) on EEG: \(\approx23\%\) of windows near-full (rel\(\le5\%\)),
\(\approx46\%\) moderate (rel\(\le20\%\)), and wrong\(>\)named on
\(\approx73\%\); it does not absorb the \(\alpha\equiv1\) gap on average
(graded Partial--Weak; Fig.~\ref{fig:alpha}d). Closure depth is therefore
regime-dependent (Table~\ref{tab:domains}), not a universal \(\alpha\rightarrow\)RDG
law.

\paragraph{What is not claimed.}
Exploratory clinical associations of EEG leftovers with cognition (AHEPA
Spearman \(\rho=-0.24\), \(n=31\), \(p=0.19\); LEMON null against healthy aging)
are deferred to Supplementary Note~\ref{snote:clinical} and are

\section*{Discussion}
% =============================================================================

This work thickens a single research line rather than stacking unrelated
applications. A Huygens-type local propagator is both \emph{task-useful} on
\stead{} (Table~\ref{tab:stead}) and \emph{scientifically productive} as the
coordinate that organises five frozen coda facies and path residuals
(Fig.~\ref{fig:seismic}). Cross-domain ablations show that the same primitive
lands elsewhere at instantaneous and graph-diffusion coordinates, and
\(\Theta(\lambda)\) supplies a continuous bridge with a damped-wave phase
(Fig.~\ref{fig:cross}). Validated \(\alpha\) then measures local departure from
those landings, with closure depth itself regime-dependent
(Fig.~\ref{fig:alpha}).

Huygens is therefore the right \emph{interpretable computational principle for
seismology} in this study---not a universal law of cortex or ocean. The five
facies classify how paths ring under that principle; they are not earthquake
clocks or precursors. The classical-coda geography control clarifies that the
map leftover is not a mysterious neural-only field, while still elevating facies
as an interpretable discrete language for path structure under an operational
picker that times coda windows without a separate catalog.

Limitations are intrinsic to the present evidence. Taxonomy gates beyond
synthetic recovery (T2--T4) remain open. Cascadia supplies consistency examples
without a complete spatial multiple-testing catalog; Berg/Lin cubes are
unavailable there. SST physics maps are sensor-graph scale, not Rd. EEG clinical
leftovers are exploratory and conventionally non-significant on the primary
Spearman; LEMON is a healthy negative control, not a second clinical site. A
Physics Decoder attached to frozen seismic latents improves travel-time misfit
as an initialisation aid on a reported protocol (Supplementary
Note~\ref{snote:decoder}) but is not a central discovery claim. Multi-primitive
mixtures are paused. Future work can extend \(\Theta\) with reaction, advection
and richer damped-wave terms without rewriting the training skeleton.

\section*{Methods}
% =============================================================================

\paragraph{Unified operator \(P(X;G,\Theta,\alpha)\).}
Let \(X\in\mathbb{R}^{N\times T}\) be the local field on \(N\) sensors over
\(T\) time steps. Geometry \(G\) supplies pairwise distances \(r_{ij}\) and
sampling \(\mathrm{d}t\). Mechanism coordinates
\[
\Theta=(\delta,\sigma,\eta,\nu,\zeta;\gamma,\ell,c,\ldots)
\]
control delay scale \(\delta\in[0,1]\), spatial mix \(\sigma\)
(wave-amplitude \(\leftrightarrow\) graph-exponential), heat mix \(\eta\)
(toward continuum heat), norm mix \(\nu\) (column\(\leftrightarrow\)row), and
travel-time damping \(\zeta\ge0\), with auxiliary scales
\((\gamma,\ell,c,\ldots)\) matched to legacy priors at named corners. Geometric
travel time is \(\tau_{\mathrm{geo},ij}=\mathrm{round}(r_{ij}/(c\,\mathrm{d}t))\);
lags are \(\tau_{ij}=\mathrm{round}(\delta\cdot\tau_{\mathrm{geo},ij})\).
Unnormalized amplitudes blend wave-like
\(e^{-\gamma r^{2}}/(r+\varepsilon)\), graph \(e^{-r/\ell}\) and heat-kernel
shapes; optional damping multiplies \(e^{-\zeta\tau_{\mathrm{geo}}}\). After
normalisation, neighbour drive is aggregated with lag \(\tau_{ij}\) and passed
with the self state through a shared local response \(R\) to produce the next
field. Reality uses \(A\odot\alpha\). Named corners recover legacy
\stead{}/EEG/SST priors: the \texttt{exact} backend delegates to established
kernels; the \texttt{unified} backend matches within numerical tolerance
(\texttt{unified\_special\_case\_v1/}). Code:
\texttt{hnf/propagation\_dynamics/unified/}.

\paragraph{Named corners and \(\Theta(\lambda)\).}
Canonical corners (after clamping): wave/Huygens
\((\delta,\sigma,\eta,\zeta)\approx(1,0,0,0)\); instantaneous
\((\delta{=}0,\sigma{=}0)\); graph diffusion
\((\delta{=}0,\sigma{=}1,\eta{=}0)\); continuum diffusion raises \(\eta\).
Telegrapher path: \(\delta=(1-\lambda)^{2}\), \(\eta=\lambda^{2}\),
\(\zeta=4\lambda(1-\lambda)\), with spatial mix interpolating the
wave\(\leftrightarrow\)heat family. Continuity gates require finite amp/MSE
steps, monotone lag decrease on wave\(\rightarrow\)diffusion, and visitation of
wave-like and diffusive ends; damped-wave is recorded when mid-path damping
peaks (\texttt{unified\_theta\_lambda\_v1/}; primary lag \(0.523\rightarrow0\)).
Multi-primitive mixtures \(\sum_k\alpha_k K_k\) are paused and unused.

\paragraph{Matched-budget regime ablation.}
For EEG and SST, regimes \(\{\texttt{wave},\texttt{instantaneous},
\texttt{diffusion},\texttt{graph\_diffusion}\}\) are trained under a shared
protocol: identical local-response capacity, identical optimisation budget
(steps / early-stopping patience), and a shared freeze policy on \(R\) when
comparing priors. Geometry controls scramble or refit against wrong distances
while holding capacity fixed; preferred \(\Theta^{\star}\) is the lowest-MSE
prior that remains geometry-sensitive under those controls. Empirical
\(\alpha\equiv1\) instances under the unified operator
(\texttt{unified\_empirical\_instances\_v1/}) must recover the same preferred
labels with exact/unified backend parity. Synthetic taxonomy gate T1
(\texttt{synth\_regime\_ident\_v1/}) recovers known ground-truth regimes at
confusion accuracy \(1.0\); T2--T4 remain open.

\paragraph{\stead{} Huygens probe and timing benchmark.}
The production seismic probe is a multi-scale Huygens--Fresnel \pnf{}
(\(\approx192\,\mathrm{k}\) parameters). Inputs are three-component \stead{}
windows resampled to length \(800\) covering \(60\,\mathrm{s}\) (native
\(100\,\mathrm{Hz}\)). A source embedding (width \(64\)) feeds fine/coarse
WaveBlocks (causal windows \(8\,\mathrm{s}\) / \(20\,\mathrm{s}\); three blocks
per scale) with Softplus-constrained kernel parameters \((\gamma,\omega,c)\);
\(c\) is treated as a support scale, not an identified medium velocity.
Detection and P/S heads decode on the discrete \(800\)-bin grid (bin width
\(\approx0.075\,\mathrm{s}\)). Training: Adam with cosine annealing; batch
size \(32\); \(\approx50\) epochs (best validation near epoch \(37\)); loss
combines detection/P/S cross-entropy with order and mild physical regularisers
as in the seismic draft. Full-test evaluation uses tolerance
\(0.5\,\mathrm{s}\) and \eqt{} pick/detection thresholds. Metrics:
detection/P/S F1 with precision/recall, and pick MAE (s). Baselines: \eqt{}
(\(\sim377\,\mathrm{k}\)), \pnet{} (\(\sim269\,\mathrm{k}\)).
\textbf{Authoritative numbers are Table~\ref{tab:stead}} (matching
\texttt{scripts/sn-article.tex}). An earlier snapshot
\texttt{outputs/paper\_stead\_full\_test\_compare/} (e.g.\ P MAE
\(0.0213\,\mathrm{s}\)) is superseded. OBS/transfer settings are not primary
claims.

\paragraph{Facies observables and frozen ceilings.}
All morphology analyses use one frozen multi-scale checkpoint. Causal-chain
exports provide (i)~coda slope from the waveform envelope in an \(S\)-timed
window, (ii)~onset sharpness, and (iii)~peak count / multi-lobe structure in
delay-time density \(\rho(\tau)\) after resampling each event into a causal
frame with \(P\) at \(\tau=0\) and \(S\) at \(\tau=1\). Labels are rule-based
(\textit{impulsive\_fast\_decay}, \textit{emergent}, \textit{multipath},
\textit{slow\_coda}, \textit{standard}), not unsupervised cluster IDs.
Ceilings (Supplementary Table~\ref{stab:ceilings}) were chosen on validation
to separate the three observables, then frozen before any test-set or regional
map inspection. Because coda slope enters the ceilings, within-class coda
contrasts are facies characterisation, not independent physical validation.
Geography uses the \stead{} local-earthquake test catalogue after noise
exclusion (\(>100{,}000\) traces); association with region is summarised by
Cram\'er's \(V\) with FDR adjustment.

\paragraph{Structure residual, independence, Cascadia, classical control.}
Coda slope is distance-detrended to \(\beta\) against
\(\log_{10}(\mathrm{dist}+1)\). A ridge expectation (\(\lambda=8\)) removes
\(\log_{10}\) distance, depth, magnitude, \(\log(1+\mathrm{fault\ distance})\)
to nearest Quaternary fault, and station fixed effects; residual
\(\beta_{\mathrm{res}}\) is mapped at path midpoints (SoCal fixed stations;
\(20\)--\(120\,\mathrm{km}\); structure model \(R^{2}\approx0.27\)).
Same-station test: in-cell versus out-of-cell paths at the same station with
geographic buffer; discovery requires \(\ge8\) stations and \(p<0.05\).
Classical single-trace \(q_c\) uses a mean-square envelope, 21-sample smoother,
and the slope of \(\log_{10}E(t)\) from the first post-peak drop below
\(55\%\) of peak energy through \(+8\,\mathrm{s}\). Berg upper-crust \(V_S\)
(\(0\)--\(8\,\mathrm{km}\) column mean) and Lin \& Jordan direct-wave \(Q_S\)
(resolved nodes only) are sampled at midpoints. Cascadia uses the identical
frozen ceilings and ridge; jackknives (source-bin / magnitude / year) are
STABLE if \(\ge80\%\) of qualifying tests keep the expected \(\Delta\beta\)
sign with \(p<0.05\) and \(n_{\mathrm{sites}}\ge8\). Catalog-timed classical
control: identical envelope slope with \stead{} catalog \(S\) on \(n=4000\)
SoCal traces; five-fold OOS \(R^{2}\) and \(\Delta R^{2}\) for probe latents as
in Results. Joint OOS residualisation on Berg/Lin summaries remains
\(\lesssim0.01\) (random folds can still admit spatial leakage).

\paragraph{\(\alpha\) gates (EEG harden, LEMON, SST/EEG RDG).}
\(\alpha\) is mined only under the domain-preferred prior. EEG instantaneous
harden (\texttt{eeg\_instantaneous\_harden\_v1/}; five seeds; subject
bootstrap) reports: G1 \(\alpha\) vs \(\alpha\equiv1\); G2 channel-shuffle;
G3 geometry-scramble refit; G4 CF \(\alpha\) evaluated on wrong geometry;
G6 permute \(\alpha\) edges; G7 distance-slope sign stability across seeds.
LEMON replication (R1--R4 on MPI-LEMON) repeats preferred-regime and key CF
gates on an independent healthy cohort. SST RDG (H1c\(^\prime\),
\(\ell=0.5\)): mine a range-dependent gain on one seed, \emph{freeze} the
functional form, evaluate held-out seeds with named versus wrong/sign-flip
reinjection, and \textbf{forbid} mechanism-parameter refit at reinjection.
EEG RDG uses the same atom family and is graded Partial--Weak when near-full /
moderate / wrong\(>\)named fractions fail SST-level closure. Unfair free/local
MSE baselines do not replace CF mechanism gates; fair distance-only scramble
follow-ups are noted in Extended Data.

\paragraph{Statistics.}
Same-station \(\Delta\beta\): site-median effect, Cohen's \(d\), Mann--Whitney
\(p\). Facies--geography: Cram\'er's \(V\) with FDR. EEG gates:
subject-bootstrap intervals on relative CF degradation. Clinical leftover
Spearman (Supplementary Note~\ref{snote:clinical}) is two-sided; shuffle and
capacity-matched nulls are complementary and do not replace the association
\(p\)-value.

\subsection*{Supplementary Note: Physics Decoder (not a main claim)}
\label{snote:decoder}
A lightweight Physics Decoder (2-layer MLP on frozen \pnf{} latents) maps probe
features to layered \((v_P,v_S)\) models used to initialise differentiable FWI.
On the reported protocol, \(69.6\%\) of real \stead{} events (\(n=500\)) improve
relative to perturbed baselines (mean travel-time misfit \(11.22\rightarrow3.08\)).
Layered ground-truth provenance, station pooling, and geographic-prior controls
are only partially documented; the decoder is an initialisation aid, not a
central discovery claim.

\subsection*{Supplementary Note: Exploratory clinical EEG leftover (not a biomarker)}
\label{snote:clinical}
On AHEPA (\(n=88\) HC/FTD/AD), a train-only residualizer isolates a diffusion-probe
leftover against classical voltmeter features. Primary held-out Spearman with
MMSE residual is \(\rho=-0.24\) (\(n=31\), \(p=0.19\)); Pearson \(r=-0.30\) on
the same pool. Train-shuffle / capacity-matched nulls yield mean
\(|\rho|\approx0.05\)--\(0.07\) with \(p_{\ge\mathrm{obs}}\approx0.002\) and do
\emph{not} replace the association \(p\)-value. On independent LEMON adults
(\(n=202\)) the leftover is null against healthy aging, GM/ICV and TMT. We do
not claim a clinical biomarker.

\subsection*{Supplementary Note: Explicit non-claims}
\begin{itemize}
\item No universal Huygens law for cortex or ocean.
\item No clinical EEG biomarker; no AD/FTD classifier product.
\item No claim that \(\beta_{\mathrm{res}}\) is uniquely neural beyond classical
  catalog-timed coda geography.
\item No \(\alpha\) as classical seismic travel-time residual tomography.
\item No multi-primitive mixture \(\sum_k\alpha_k K_k\) (paused).
\item No uniform SOTA across OBS/transfer; Table~\ref{tab:stead} is full-test
  usefulness under an interpretable prior.
\item Taxonomy T2--T4 not closed.
\end{itemize}
