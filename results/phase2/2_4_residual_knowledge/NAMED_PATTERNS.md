# Residual pattern hunt — can we name new knowledge?

**Short answer: no new geophysical law; yes to model phenomenology.**

## What looks like a pattern

1. **Corrective residual (strong, expected)**  
   Refine shift opposes physics-proposal timing bias: STEAD 98.0% / ETHZ 94.8% of windows; Spearman(prop_err, refine_shift) ≈ −0.50 / −0.59.  
   This shows the residual is structured and useful — not a new Earth law.

2. **|refine| shrinks as P–S gap grows (moderate, cross-domain)**  
   Controlling for SNR: STEAD ρ≈−0.45, ETHZ ρ≈−0.15 (same sign).  
   Tree rule: short P–S (≲3.3 s) → larger |ref|.  
   Still confounded with near-event geometry; do not promote to “new physics.”

3. **|refine| vs distance / SNR**  
   Present on STEAD; partly redundant with P–S / difficulty. Descriptive only.

## What we did *not* find

- A residual motif that predicts a **held-out physical quantity unused in training** better than SNR/distance baselines.  
- A law that survives de-confounding and would surprise a seismologist.

## Bottom line for TGRS

- Keep: unnamed irreducible timing information + corrective refine behavior.  
- Optional one sentence: residual magnitude decreases with P–S gap (SNR-controlled), STEAD→ETHZ same sign.  
- Do **not** claim discovery of new seismological knowledge from the residual.
